The core exists.

# How to Read This Page

Different parts of Zipvilization are at very different stages.

A concept can be completely defined without being live.

A system can be built without having real data yet.

An architecture can be ready while its final infrastructure remains deliberately undecided.

And some parts of Zipvilization are designed to continue evolving.

For that reason, this page does not use a single percentage to describe the entire project.

Instead, it distinguishes between four different states:

**DEFINED**  
The rules, architecture or identity are established.

**BUILT**  
A functional implementation exists.

**LIVE**  
The component is operating with real Zipvilization state.

**EVOLVING**  
The component exists, but its nature is expected to continue developing.

> **DEFINED ≠ BUILT ≠ LIVE**

Understanding that distinction is essential when reading the Atlas.

# TGE / Genesis

**STATUS: TECHNICALLY READY / WAITING FOR PARTICIPATION**

There is currently no fixed Genesis date.

This is not because Genesis is waiting for a missing core technology.

Zipvilization could technically execute the TGE now.

We are waiting for something else:

**Colonists.**

The objective is to begin with a sufficiently broad group of independent participants and wallets.

We do not want to manufacture urgency around that process.

We do not have a deadline that forces us to launch.

And we are not in a hurry.

> **Genesis will not happen because a marketing calendar says it should.**

It will happen when we consider the initial participation conditions appropriate for Zipvilization to begin.

# Why Is Genesis Designed This Way?

The initial Pool is intended to contain the complete SOLUM supply paired with only approximately **$100 equivalent in ETH**.

That is intentional.

Genesis is not designed as a fundraising event.

The initial conditions are designed so that Founding Colonists can participate with an extremely low economic barrier.

Under the intended initial conditions, the objective is for the economic cost of executing a MAX BUY for early Founding Colonists to remain below approximately $1 in SOLUM.

Depending on network and platform costs, some participants may spend more on transaction and platform fees than on the SOLUM itself.

Actual acquisition conditions will depend on the AMM state and prior interactions.

The objective is not to use Founding Colonists to artificially inflate the initial price.

The objective is distribution and participation.

> **We don't need your capital.**
>
> **We need your participation.**

# SolumWorld

**STATUS: ARCHITECTURE DEFINED / DATA-DEPENDENT**
