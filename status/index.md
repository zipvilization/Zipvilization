# Status Key

The following terms are used deliberately.

## DEFINED

The concept, rule or relationship has been canonically specified.

`DEFINED ≠ DEVELOPED`

---

## DEVELOPED

A technical implementation or substantial working system exists.

`DEVELOPED ≠ TESTED`

---

## TESTED

The system has undergone development testing, automated testing, deployment testing or other relevant validation.

`TESTED ≠ LIVE`

---

## LIVE

The official production system or canonical state is active.

`LIVE ≠ MATURE`

---

## MATURE

A live system or world state has reached the maturity conditions defined for it.

---

## DATA_DEPENDENT

The system is defined or developed, but its meaningful operation or representation requires real canonical data or sufficient History.

`DATA_DEPENDENT ≠ MISSING`

---

## EXPERIMENTAL

The system, implementation or behavior is being explored and may change.

`EXPERIMENTAL ≠ CANONICAL`

---

## OPEN

The final form or outcome is intentionally not predetermined.

`OPEN ≠ MISSING`
