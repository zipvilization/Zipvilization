# Principles

Every civilization is shaped by its principles.

Technology changes.

Communities evolve.

Even the people who begin a project eventually step aside.

Principles remain.

Zipvilization is built upon a small number of principles that guide every decision, every rule and every future evolution.

---

## Participation before investment

Zipvilization is a project of participation.

Not of speculation.

People are invited to understand the experiment before deciding whether to become Colonists.

Participation is the foundation.

Investment is always a personal decision.

Learn more →

## Honesty before marketing

Zipvilization does not promise success.

It does not promise profit.

It does not promise certainty.

It offers an experiment.

Every person should be free to understand it and decide whether to participate.

---

## Public rules

A civilization cannot depend on hidden decisions.

Its rules should be public, understandable and verifiable.

Technology exists to preserve those rules.

## Understanding before action

The first act of participation is understanding.

This Atlas exists to help Humans and Artificial Intelligence understand the project before making any decision.

Knowledge comes first.

Everything else comes afterwards.
