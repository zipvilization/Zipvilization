# Solum

Every civilization begins with land.

Not with money.

Not with technology.

With territory.

Solum is the territorial foundation of Zipvilization.

It is the soil upon which the civilization can emerge.

Everything else exists because Solum exists.

---

## A world waiting to awaken

Before the first Colonist arrives...

Before the first Farm appears...

Before history begins...

The world already exists.

It is silent.

Dormant.

Full of potential.

Solum represents that world before civilization.

It is not empty.

It is waiting.

---

## Three possible states

Every part of Solum belongs to one of three possible states.

### Dormant Land

Most of the world begins here.

Dormant land has not yet become part of the civilization.

It patiently awaits future Colonists.

Every new participant awakens another small part of the world.

In the smart contract this state is technically managed through the **Pool**.

---

### Living Civilization

As Colonists participate, dormant land becomes living territory.

Farms emerge.

Cities grow.

States organize.

Kingdoms connect regions.

The civilization does not appear instantly.

It awakens gradually.

---

### Permanent Nature

Not every place should become civilization.

Some forests should always remain forests.

Some rivers should always remain rivers.

Some mountains should never belong to anyone.

Permanent Nature protects the identity of the world.

Within the smart contract this permanence is technically implemented through the **Burn** mechanism.

In Zipvilization, Burn does not represent destruction.

It represents preservation.

---

## Participation transforms the world

Colonists do not simply acquire territory.

They awaken dormant land.

Every new Colonist changes the state of the world.

The civilization grows because people choose to participate.

Not because new territory is created.

Nothing new is added.

The same world simply becomes more alive.

---

## Public rules

This transformation is not controlled by people.

It is governed by public rules.

Every transition between Dormant Land, Living Civilization and Permanent Nature follows deterministic rules defined by the protocol.

Those rules are implemented by the smart contract and can be verified by anyone.

## Connected Concepts

Solum is one layer of a larger system.
