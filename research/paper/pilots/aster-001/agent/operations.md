# Aster — Operations

## Normal Operation

Sensors record measurements and submit them every 10 minutes.

Measurement values use SI units.

## Connectivity Loss

Sensors continue recording observations while connectivity is unavailable.

Those observations are stored locally.

When connectivity returns, pending observations may be sent together rather than individually.

Delayed transmission must remain distinguishable from measurement time.
