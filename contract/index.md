# 📜 The Contract

Every civilization is shaped by its laws.

In Zipvilization, those laws begin with a **public smart contract**.

The contract is not simply a technical component.

It is the foundation upon which the entire civilization is built.

Everything that happens inside Zipvilization begins here.

---

## 🌍 More Than Code

Most blockchain projects use smart contracts to manage digital assets.

Zipvilization uses a smart contract to establish the rules of an evolving world.

The contract does not create stories.

It creates conditions.

It defines how territory exists.

How it moves.

How it matures.

How participants interact with it.

Everything else emerges from those rules.

---

## ⚖️ The Laws of the World

The contract establishes the immutable foundations of Zipvilization.

Among them are:

- territorial supply;
- ownership;
- transfers;
- liquidity;
- market interaction;
- transaction fees;
- initial participation limits;
- and every rule required for the civilization to begin.

The contract does not decide outcomes.

It defines the conditions under which outcomes may emerge.

---

## 🔍 Public and Verifiable

Every rule can be inspected.

Every transaction can be verified.

Every participant observes the same contract.

Nothing depends on hidden databases or private decisions.

Anyone can audit the code.

Anyone can verify the execution.

The civilization begins with transparency.

---

## 🌱 Why Does It Matter?

Without the contract, there is no shared territory.

Without shared territory, there are no Colonists.

Without Colonists, there is no civilization.

The contract is therefore much more than software.

It is the common framework that allows every participant to observe and interact with the same world.

---

## 🚀 Designed for Phase 0

The initial version of the contract is intentionally focused on one objective:

**Activate the experiment.**

Its responsibility is not to define every future Chapter.

Its responsibility is to establish the initial rules from which the civilization can begin to grow.

Future Chapters may expand the possibilities of Zipvilization.

The contract begins by creating a stable foundation.
