# 🪙 Solum

Every civilization begins with territory.

Before there are Colonists...

Before there are Farms, Cities or Kingdoms...

There must be land.

In Zipvilization, that land is represented by **Solum**.

---

## 🌍 Territory First

Solum is not the purpose of Zipvilization.

It is the foundation.

Every unit of Solum represents a portion of the world's territory.

Owning Solum means administering part of the land from which the civilization may emerge.

Without territory there can be no settlement.

Without settlement there can be no civilization.

---

## 📏 One Unit, One Square Metre

Zipvilization uses a simple territorial model.

> **1 Solum = 1 square metre of territory.**

This relationship never changes.

It allows every participant to understand exactly what each unit represents.

The blockchain records balances.

Zipvilization interprets those balances as territory.

---

## ⛰️ A Finite World

The territory of Zipvilization is fixed.

No additional land can ever be created.

The complete world exists from the beginning.

Colonists do not create territory.

They gradually occupy and administer it.

This makes every part of the world part of the same finite geography.

---

## 🔒 Immutable Supply

The total supply of Solum is defined when the contract is deployed.

After deployment:

- no new territory can be minted;
- no inflation can expand the world;
- every Colonist shares the same finite territory.

The size of the world remains constant.

Only its distribution changes.

---

## 🌱 Territory Is Potential

Owning territory does not immediately create a civilization.

Territory provides potential.

Time transforms that potential into population.

Population allows Farms, Cities, States and Kingdoms to emerge.

Solum therefore represents much more than ownership.

It represents the possibility of future development.

---

## ⚖️ Territory Changes Hands

As Colonists interact with the market, territory moves between participants.

The amount of territory remains the same.

Only its administration changes.

Each transfer becomes part of the observable history of the civilization.

The world is constant.

Its inhabitants are not.

---

## 🌍 Why Solum Exists

Zipvilization could not exist without a common territorial layer.

Solum provides that layer.

It allows every participant to observe, acquire, transfer and administer exactly the same finite world.

The contract guarantees the rules.

The blockchain preserves the history.

Solum connects both.

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> **Solum is not simply a token.**
>
> **It is the digital representation of the territory from which the civilization may emerge.**
