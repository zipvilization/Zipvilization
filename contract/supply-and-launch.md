# 📦 Supply & Launch

Every world needs a beginning.

In Zipvilization, that beginning is defined at the moment the smart contract is deployed.

From the very first block, the entire territory already exists.

The civilization does not.

Only the possibility of one.

---

## 🌍 A Complete World

Unlike physical civilizations, Zipvilization never discovers new land.

Its geography is complete from the beginning.

Every square metre already exists.

Every future Farm, City, State and Kingdom already belongs somewhere within that territory.

The experiment is not about creating more land.

It is about observing how that land becomes inhabited over time.

---

## 🪙 Fixed Supply

The total amount of Solum is permanently defined when the contract is deployed.

No additional territory can ever be created.

No authority can expand the world.

No future Chapter can increase the total supply.

The geography of Zipvilization remains constant.

Only its distribution changes.

---

## 🚫 No Minting

After deployment, no new Solum can be minted.

This guarantees that every Colonist participates under the same territorial rules.

Future generations will inherit the same world.

Not a larger one.

Not a smaller one.

The experiment begins with one complete territory.

It remains that way forever.

---

## 🌊 The First Distribution

At launch, the territory has not yet been colonized.

It exists.

It is available.

It is waiting.

As participation begins, portions of that territory leave the initial Pool and become administered by the first Colonists.

The geography does not change.

Its occupation does.

---

## 🚀 Phase 0

The launch of the contract does not create a civilization.

It creates the conditions required for one to begin.

Phase 0 exists to allow:

- the first Colonists to arrive;
- the first territory to be distributed;
- the first maturation cycles to begin;
- the first Farms to emerge;
- and the first observable history to be written.

The contract starts the world.

The Colonists start the civilization.

---

## ⏳ The Beginning of History

At launch, every territory shares the same starting point.

None has population.

None has history.

None has matured.

History begins only when Colonists start interacting with the world.

Each acquisition.

Each transfer.

Each block.

Each cycle.

Together they become the first chapter of the civilization.

---

## 🌱 Why It Matters

A fixed territorial supply creates a shared world.

Every participant knows that:

- everyone lives inside the same geography;
- no hidden territory can appear;
- no authority can expand the land;
- every future civilization develops within the same finite world.

The experiment is therefore based on permanence.

Not expansion.

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> **Zipvilization begins with a complete world.**
>
> **The experiment is not about creating territory.**
>
> **It is about allowing that territory to become a civilization.**
