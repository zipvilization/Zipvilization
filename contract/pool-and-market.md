# 🌊 Pool & Market

Every civilization begins with unoccupied land.

In Zipvilization, that land exists inside the **initial Pool**.

The Pool is more than liquidity.

It represents the territory that has not yet been colonized.

---

## 🌍 A World Waiting for Its First Colonists

When the smart contract is deployed, the entire territory already exists.

But none of it has an administrator.

No Farms exist.

No Cities exist.

No population has appeared.

The world is complete.

The civilization has not yet begun.

The Pool represents that initial state.

---

## 🤝 From the Pool to the Civilization

Each time a Colonist acquires Solum, something more than a market transaction occurs.

A portion of the world's territory leaves the Pool.

It becomes administered.

Its maturation begins.

Its history begins.

The Pool becomes slightly smaller.

The civilization becomes slightly larger.

---

## 📈 The Market Is Part of the Experiment

The market distributes territory.

It does not define the civilization.

People may buy.

People may sell.

People may transfer territory.

These interactions are expected.

They are part of the experiment.

What matters is not only the transaction itself.

It is the long-term consequences that every transaction produces inside the world.

---

## 🌱 Every Transaction Changes the World

Every interaction creates change.

A purchase changes territorial administration.

A transfer begins a new relationship between a Colonist and a territory.

A sale makes territory available for someone else.

The geography remains the same.

Its administration evolves continuously.

This is how the civilization develops.

---

## ⏳ Distribution Before Concentration

During Phase 0, the objective is not to concentrate territory.

It is to distribute it.

A civilization becomes richer when many Colonists participate.

For this reason, the initial design encourages broad participation and limits excessive territorial accumulation.

These limits belong to the beginning of the experiment.

They protect the diversity of its first generation.

---

## 🔄 A Living Territory

The Pool is not static.

Its role changes as the civilization evolves.

At first, it contains almost the entire world.

As Colonists participate, more territory becomes part of the civilization.

Over time, the relationship between the Pool, the market and the Colonists tells the story of how the world became inhabited.

---

## 🌍 More Than Liquidity

Traditional liquidity pools exist to facilitate trading.

The Pool of Zipvilization has an additional meaning.

It represents territory that still belongs to the common world.

As long as land remains inside the Pool, it has not yet entered the observable history of the civilization.

Every acquisition writes a small new chapter.

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> **The Pool is not only liquidity.**
>
> **It is the uncolonized territory of Zipvilization.**
