# ⚖️ Taxes

Every action has consequences.

Zipvilization follows the same principle.

Every purchase.

Every sale.

Every transfer.

Each interaction leaves a trace that extends beyond the transaction itself.

Taxes are one of the mechanisms that make those consequences possible.

---

## 🌍 More Than a Fee

In many blockchain projects, transaction taxes exist primarily to finance development or reward holders.

Zipvilization approaches them differently.

Taxes are part of the world's design.

They connect economic activity with the evolution of the civilization.

Every interaction contributes to shaping the world.

---

## 🔄 Every Transaction Changes the World

A transaction is never an isolated event.

When territory changes hands, the civilization changes too.

Administration changes.

History changes.

Future maturation changes.

The market records an exchange.

The civilization records a new chapter.

Taxes are one of the mechanisms that ensure every interaction has lasting consequences.

---

## 🌱 Balance Instead of Extraction

The objective of the tax system is not simply to collect value.

Its purpose is to maintain balance.

The contract can redistribute part of each interaction toward different components of the world.

Depending on the design of each Chapter, taxes may influence:

- the Pool;
- permanent territory;
- the long-term sustainability of the system;
- and future mechanisms that have not yet been activated.

The important idea is simple.

Transactions do not exist outside the civilization.

They become part of it.

---

## ⏳ Every Block Builds History

The blockchain records a transaction.

The contract executes its rules.

The taxes apply their consequences.

Time preserves the result.

Each interaction becomes another piece of the world's history.

The civilization evolves one block at a time.

---

## 🌍 A Living Economy

Zipvilization does not separate economics from the world.

The economy is one of the forces that shape it.

Buying territory.

Selling territory.

Transferring territory.

All of these actions influence the evolution of the civilization.

The market is therefore not external to Zipvilization.

It is one of the systems through which the civilization changes over time.

---

## 🚀 Designed for the Long Term

The first implementation of the tax system supports the objectives of Phase 0.

Future Chapters may introduce new mechanisms, new destinations for fees and new forms of interaction.

Those changes should never alter the core philosophy.

Every transaction must continue to produce meaningful consequences inside the world.

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> **Taxes are not simply collected.**
>
> **They transform every interaction into part of the history of the civilization.**
