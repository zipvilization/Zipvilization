# 🛡️ Fair Access

Every civilization has a beginning.

The conditions at that beginning matter.

Zipvilization introduces **Fair Access** during **Phase 0** to encourage a broad and diverse first generation of Colonists.

These rules are not designed to control the market.

They are designed to protect the initial conditions of the experiment.

---

---

## 🌱 Why Are There Limits?

The objective of Phase 0 is not to distribute as much territory as possible to as few participants as possible.

The objective is to allow the civilization to begin with many independent Colonists.

A broader territorial distribution creates richer initial conditions.

The experiment benefits from diversity.

---

## 🌍 Distribution Before Concentration

At launch, almost the entire world is still waiting to be colonized.

The first interactions define the initial geography of the civilization.

For this reason, the contract encourages territorial distribution before large-scale concentration.

This is not presented as a permanent economic rule.

It belongs to the beginning of the experiment.

---

## 👥 Maximum Territory per Colonist

During Phase 0, the contract limits the maximum amount of territory that a single wallet can initially administer.

This mechanism exists to reduce excessive early concentration and to encourage participation from many independent Colonists.

Every wallet begins under the same public rules.

The exact limit is defined in the smart contract.

---

## 🔄 Maximum Transaction Size

The contract also limits the maximum amount of territory that can be acquired in a single transaction.

This encourages a more gradual distribution of territory during the first stages of the civilization.

Rather than allowing a small number of very large acquisitions, the system favours a progressive occupation of the world.

---

## 🌟 Early Adopters

The first generation of Colonists occupies a unique place in the history of Zipvilization.

There can only be one first generation.

Fair Access seeks to make that generation as representative as possible.

Its purpose is not to maximise investment.

Its purpose is to maximise participation.

If the experiment succeeds, the first Colonists will always remain the first witnesses to the birth of the civilization.

---

## ⏳ Dynamic Rules

Fair Access is designed for **Phase 0**.

As Zipvilization evolves, some participation rules may evolve as well.

Future Chapters may introduce new limits, remove existing ones or adapt them to the maturity of the civilization.

The initial conditions are designed for the beginning.

Not necessarily for every future stage.

---

## 📜 Public by Design

Fair Access is implemented directly in the smart contract.

The rules are:

- public;
- deterministic;
- verifiable;
- identical for every participant.

No administrator decides who may participate.

The contract applies the same rules to everyone.

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> **Fair Access does not protect the market.**
>
> **It protects the initial conditions required for a civilization to emerge.**
