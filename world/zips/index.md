# Zips

## The Native Inhabitants of Zipvilization

Zips are the native inhabitants of **Zipvilization**.

They are not wallet holders.

They are not Colonists.

They are not decorative avatars.

They are not arbitrary characters placed on top of the world.

They are deterministic digital individuals generated by the biological architecture of the system.

Their existence emerges from the relationship between:

**territory,**

**Farms,**

**Bloch,**

**digital genetics,**

and:

**Time.**

Conceptually:

    TERRITORY
    ↓
    FARM
    ↓
    BLOCH
    ↓
    DIGITAL GENETICS
    ↓
    BIOLOGICAL TIME
    ↓
    ZIP

A Zip therefore belongs to the world because the world generated it.

---

# The Biological Unit Begins With the Farm

The biological architecture of the Zips begins with the smallest active territorial unit:

> **1 Farm**

A Farm contains:

> **8 Tiles**

Each Tile represents:

> **1,000,000 SOLUM**

Therefore:

> **1 Farm = 8 Tiles = 8,000,000 SOLUM = 8 km²**

This threshold matters biologically.

Below it:

    SOLUM < 8,000,000
    ↓
    NO COMPLETE FARM
    ↓
    NO ACTIVE BLOCH
    ↓
    NO ZIP GENERATION

At or above it:

    SOLUM ≥ 8,000,000
    ↓
    FARM
    ↓
    BLOCH
    ↓
    BIOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT

This is the transition from passive territorial possession to active biological territory.

---

# One Farm = One Bloch

Each generating Farm contains:

> **1 Bloch**

Bloch is the biological process through which Zip generation occurs.

It is not a separate token.

It is not a second asset.

It is not an independent creature.

It is the biological state machine of the Farm.

Conceptually:

    FARM
    ↓
    BLOCH
    ↓
    BIOLOGICAL TIME
    ↓
    ZIP GENERATION

The Farm provides territorial capacity.

Bloch converts that capacity into biological development through Time.

---

# One Tile = Capacity for One Zip

Each Farm contains:

> **8 Tiles**

Each Tile provides biological capacity for:

> **1 Zip**

Therefore:

> **1 complete Farm has capacity for 8 Zips**

But:

> **Capacity ≠ Population**

A newly formed Farm does not instantly contain eight Zips.

The population emerges through biological Time.

This distinction is fundamental.

Territory defines:

> **how many Zips can exist**

Time determines:

> **when they emerge**

---

# Biological Time

Zip generation occurs through biological cycles.

The canonical cycle length is:

> **65,536 blocks**

This value is not arbitrary.

It corresponds to:

> **2¹⁶ blocks**

One biological cycle therefore contains:

    65,536 BLOCKS

A generating Farm can produce:

> **1 Zip per biological cycle**

provided the relevant conditions remain valid.

Conceptually:

    GENERATING FARM
    ↓
    65,536 BLOCKS
    ↓
    1 BIOLOGICAL CYCLE
    ↓
    1 ZIP

The Farm does not generate its full population immediately.

It matures.

---

# Eight Cycles Complete the Farm

A Farm contains:

> **8 Tiles**

Each Tile provides capacity for one Zip.

One biological cycle can generate one Zip.

Therefore:

> **8 biological cycles are required to populate a complete Farm**

Conceptually:

    CYCLE 1 → ZIP 1
    CYCLE 2 → ZIP 2
    CYCLE 3 → ZIP 3
    CYCLE 4 → ZIP 4
    CYCLE 5 → ZIP 5
    CYCLE 6 → ZIP 6
    CYCLE 7 → ZIP 7
    CYCLE 8 → ZIP 8

After eight valid cycles:

    FARM
    ↓
    8 TILES
    ↓
    8 ZIPS
    ↓
    COMPLETE FARM POPULATION

This gives the Farm a natural biological maturation period.

---

Each Zip corresponds conceptually to:

> **1 bit**

A complete Farm contains capacity for:

> **8 Zips**

Therefore:

    8 ZIPS
    =
    8 BITS

    1 BYTE

> **1 complete populated Farm = 1 byte**

Conceptually:

    FARM
    ↓
    8 TILES
    ↓
    8 ZIPS
    ↓
    8 BITS
    ↓
    1 BYTE

The biological architecture therefore mirrors the digital nature of the world.

    1 TILE

    CAPACITY FOR 1 ZIP
    =
    1 BIOLOGICAL BIT

    8 TILES
    =

> a territorial unit

It is also:

This relationship connects:

- territory;

- biological Time.

# Population Is Not Instantaneous

The existence of territorial capacity does not imply immediate biological completion.

A Farm may exist while containing:

- 0 Zips;
- 1 Zip;
- 2 Zips;
- 3 Zips;
- ...
- 8 Zips.

The exact population depends on:

- the age of the Farm;
- the number of completed biological cycles;
- whether the relevant conditions remained valid.

Therefore:

    FARM EXISTS
    ≠
    FARM FULLY POPULATED

This distinction allows the world to develop visibly through Time.

> **territorial maturity**

> **a single linear population generator**

This distinction is important.

    FARM

Each larger territorial structure contains many Farm-equivalent biological units.

> **Farm → Bloch → Time → Zip**

The system does not need a separate species-generation mechanism for Cities, States or Kingdoms.

It scales the Farm architecture.

    FARM A → BLOCH A → ZIP

    FARM D → BLOCH D → ZIP

# Digital Genetics

A Zip is not merely a population counter.

Each Zip is:

> **a unique digital individual**

Its identity is generated through:

> **digital genetics**

The exact implementation can evolve, but the principle is fixed:

> **Zips are deterministic individuals, not arbitrary visual outputs.**

The same underlying genetic state should always correspond to the same Zip.

This preserves identity.

---

# Deterministic Identity

Determinism is essential because a Zip must remain itself.

If the same biological state could generate a completely different individual each time it was observed, persistent identity would disappear.

Therefore:

    SAME VALID GENETIC STATE
    ↓
    SAME ZIP IDENTITY

The representation may evolve.

The identity must remain coherent.
