# Zips

Territory gives Zipvilization space.

Zips give that Territory life.

They are the native population of the world.

But a Zip is not simply a population counter.

It is not a decorative character placed over blockchain data.

And it is not an interchangeable unit waiting for a Human command.

A Zip is an individual.

Its existence emerges from deterministic conditions.

Its identity is unique.

Its development belongs to history.

And its behavior may eventually become something much less predictable.

> **The rules around a Zip can be deterministic.**
>
> **The Zip itself does not have to be.**

At the foundation of the system:

> **1 Zip = 1 bit**

Each Tile provides capacity for:

> **1 Zip**

And each complete Farm provides capacity for:

> **8 Zips**

Therefore:

> **8 Zips = 8 bits = 1 byte**

But capacity alone does not create population.

For a Zip to emerge, active Territory, a valid generating Farm, Bloch, Time and historical conditions must come together.

    TERRITORY
    ↓
    CAPACITY

    FARM
    ↓
    GENERATION RATE

    BLOCH
    ↓
    DIGITAL GENETICS

    TIME
    ↓
    PROCESS DURATION

    HISTORY
    ↓
    VALID EMERGENCE

    ZIP
    ↓
    UNIQUE INDIVIDUAL

This is where the computational architecture of Zipvilization becomes population.

# From Territory to Life

SOLUM establishes spatial capacity.

But holding SOLUM does not automatically create a Zip.

An address holding SOLUM is a:

> **Holder**

The minimum active territorial structure is:

> **1 Farm**

A Farm requires:

> **8 Tiles = 8,000,000 SOLUM = 8 km²**

Therefore:

    SOLUM < 8,000,000
    ↓
    HOLDER
    ↓
    NO COMPLETE FARM
    ↓
    NO ACTIVE COLONIST TERRITORY
    ↓
    NO BLOCH ACTIVATION
    ↓
    NO ZIP GENERATION

At the Farm threshold:

    SOLUM ≥ 8,000,000
    ↓
    1 COMPLETE FARM
    ↓
    COLONIST
    ↓
    ACTIVE TERRITORY
    ↓
    BLOCH CAN ACTIVATE
    ↓
    BIOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT CAN BEGIN

This distinction is fundamental.

> **SOLUM creates territorial potential.**
>
> **The Farm begins colonization.**
>
> **Bloch begins biological emergence.**

# One Zip, One Bit

Inside Zipvilization:

> **1 Zip = 1 bit**

This connects population with binary information.

Therefore:

> **8 Zips = 8 bits = 1 byte**

The relationship is architectural.

It does not mean that a Zip is merely a visual representation of a binary digit.

It means the same canonical population state can be expressed through two connected languages.

| Computational layer | Zipvilization |
|:--------------------|:--------------|
| 1 bit | 1 Zip |
| 8 bits | 8 Zips |
| 1 byte | 1 complete populated Farm |
| Information | Population |

This gives population a computational structure without reducing Zips to abstract numbers.

> **A Zip can be counted as information.**
>
> **But it exists in Zipvilization as an individual.**

# Bloch

Zips emerge through:

> **Bloch**

Bloch is the canonical digital-genetic mechanism associated with Zip emergence.

In the lore of Zipvilization, the Zips arrived on Solum through Bloch containers.

When valid territorial conditions activate biological development, Bloch begins its work.

The process is:

    BLOCH
    ↓
    GENERATE DIGITAL GENETIC INFORMATION
    ↓
    CONFIGURE DIGITAL GENETIC INFORMATION
    ↓
    COMPRESS DIGITAL GENETIC INFORMATION
    ↓
    UNIQUE ZIP

The final result is not a generic copy.

It is:

> **one unique individual**

This is why population generation and individual identity are related but different concepts.

The Farm determines how many generation processes can occur.

Bloch determines how each valid process becomes an individual Zip.

> **Farm defines generation rate.**
>
> **Bloch defines individual emergence.**

# Why They Are Zips

At the heart of Bloch is compression.

Digital genetic information is generated.

It is configured.

Then it is compressed into an individual.

The result is a Zip.

This gives the name a native meaning inside the world:

> **digital genetics compressed into a unique individual**

A Zip is therefore not simply spawned.

It is configured.

Compressed.

Individualized.

The population can follow deterministic mathematical limits while every valid Zip remains conceptually distinct.

# The Farm Generates Population

The Farm is the primary and persistent population-generating territorial unit.

Once valid generation is possible:

> **1 generating Farm = 1 Zip-generation process per biological cycle**

The Farm contains capacity for:

> **8 Zips**

Therefore the first Farm develops:

    1 Zip / cycle
    ×
    8 cycles
    =
    8 Zips

And:

    8 cycles
    ×
    65,536 blocks
    =
    524,288 blocks

So under continuous valid conditions:

> **Farm maturity = 8 cycles = 524,288 blocks**

At that point:

> **8 Zips = 1 byte**

But the Farm does not cease to matter.

When higher Territory becomes available, its generation capability remains part of the system.

---

# Population Does Not Stop at the Farm

A mature Farm has filled its own eight-Tile capacity.

Without additional valid Territory:

> **generation stops at that capacity**

But if valid higher territorial capacity exists, mature Farms can continue supporting Zip generation into that additional space.

This is fundamental.

Higher territorial levels do not independently generate Zips.

Instead:

> **higher Territory provides additional capacity**

while:

> **mature Farms provide the generation rate**

and:

> **Bloch produces the individual Zips**

This gives Zip population a persistent lower-level foundation.

# Capacity Is Not Population

A Colonist can hold enough SOLUM for:

> **8,192 Tiles**

That creates State-scale territorial capacity.

It does not instantly create:

> **8,192 Zips**

Population requires historical development.

The system must therefore distinguish:

- maximum Zip capacity,
- current Zip population,
- mature generating Farms,
- valid Bloch cycles,
- and historical maturity.

This distinction is essential.

> **TERRITORY CAPACITY ≠ ZIP POPULATION**
>
> **CURRENT BALANCE ≠ HISTORICAL POPULATION**

# Population Has History

A Zip that validly emerged at a particular point in blockchain history belongs to that history.

Later changes in SOLUM ownership do not make that emergence never have happened.

If a Colonist later sells or transfers Territory:

- current capacity may change,
- future generation may change,
- active territorial structure may change,

but valid previous emergence remains part of canonical history.

> **A transfer changes future conditions.**
>
> **It does not rewrite the past.**

This makes Zips historical inhabitants rather than numbers recalculated from the current balance alone.

# Zips Do Not Need Death to Balance Population

Zipvilization does not require automatic population death merely to prevent unlimited growth.

Capacity already provides the limit.

If valid Territory has capacity for eight Zips:

> **the ninth Zip cannot emerge without additional valid capacity**

If a higher territorial structure provides additional capacity, generation may continue.

If no capacity remains:

> **Bloch generation stops**

This allows the population model to remain deterministic without introducing artificial death as a balancing mechanism.

Future systems may eventually introduce additional biological or social concepts.

Those belong to Horizonte unless canonically defined.

> **Do not invent them in advance.**
