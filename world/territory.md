# 🌱 Territory

Every civilization begins with territory.

In Zipvilization, territory is more than a digital asset.

It is the physical foundation upon which the civilization can emerge.

Every Farm.

Every City.

Every State.

Every Kingdom.

Everything begins with territory.

Unlike many digital worlds, territory is never created or destroyed after deployment.

It only changes its role within the civilization.

## 🌍 A Structured World

Zipvilization does not generate territory randomly.

Every square metre belongs to a deterministic geographical structure.

Small territorial units combine into increasingly complex organizations.

As the civilization matures, territory evolves from simple settlements into larger administrative structures.

The geography remains constant.

Its organization evolves.

---

## ⛰️ Territorial Hierarchy

```
Tile
  │
  ▼
Farm
  │
  ▼
City
  │
  ▼
State
  │
  ▼
Kingdom
```

Each level represents a higher degree of territorial organization.

The objective is not simply to accumulate land.

The objective is to organize it.

---

## 🟩 Tile

A Tile is the smallest visible territorial element.

By itself it has little meaning.

Its purpose is to become part of larger territorial structures.

Tiles provide the visual foundation of the world while maintaining a deterministic relationship with the underlying territory.

---

## 🚜 Farm

A Farm is the first meaningful territorial organization.

It represents the first settled unit of the civilization.

Here, territory begins to acquire identity.

Over time, Farms mature and eventually support larger territorial structures.

---

## 🏙️ City

A City represents the first urban level of organization.

Cities emerge from mature Farms.

They become centres of population, activity and future administration.

---

## 🏛️ State

A State groups multiple Cities into a larger territorial entity.

It introduces regional organization and prepares the civilization for future administrative evolution.

---

## 👑 Kingdom

A Kingdom represents the largest territorial organization currently defined by Zipvilization.

Like every previous level, it emerges through organization, maturation and time.

Future Chapters may introduce new interactions between Kingdoms.

---

## 🌍 Every Territory Has a State

Every square metre belongs to the same finite world.

What changes is its state.

---

### 🌊 Uncolonized Territory

Territory that remains inside the **Pool** has not yet entered the civilization.

It has:

- no administrator;
- no history;
- no mature population.

It represents the world's remaining frontier.

As Colonists participate, portions of this territory leave the Pool and become part of the living civilization.

→ Learn more: **Pool & Market**

---

### 🧑‍🌾 Administered Territory

When a Colonist acquires Solum, territory leaves the Pool.

It becomes administered.

Its maturation begins.

Its history begins.

Over time it may become part of Farms, Cities, States and Kingdoms.

This is the territory where the civilization develops.

→ Learn more: **Colonists**

→ Learn more: **Population & Time**

---

### 🌿 Permanent Nature

Some territory may permanently leave the economic cycle.

Through the burn mechanism, it becomes part of the world's natural landscape.

Forests.

Rivers.

Mountains.

Protected areas.

Permanent geography.

This territory is never destroyed.

It simply adopts a different role within the world.

It no longer belongs to the market.

It belongs to Nature.

→ Learn more: **Landscape**

---

## ⏳ Territory Needs Time

Owning territory does not instantly create a Farm.

A Farm does not instantly become a City.

Territory provides potential.

Time transforms that potential into increasingly organized structures.

The geography remains constant.

Its organization evolves continuously.

---

## 🌍 One Finite World

Every Colonist participates inside the same world.

No new territory can appear.

No hidden regions exist.

No additional continents will ever be discovered.

The history of Zipvilization is therefore not the story of discovering new land.

It is the story of organizing the land that has existed since the first block.

---

## 💡 Key Idea

> **Nothing disappears.**
>
> **Everything transforms.**
>
> Territory always remains part of the world.
>
> Only its role changes.
