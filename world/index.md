# 🌍 The World

A smart contract does not create a civilization.

It creates the conditions for one.

The world of Zipvilization emerges from the interaction between **territory**, **time**, **Colonists** and **public rules**.

No single component is sufficient.

Together, they create the possibility of a living digital civilization.

---

## 🌱 Four Fundamental Elements

Every observable state of Zipvilization emerges from the interaction of four fundamental elements.

### 🌍 Territory

Every civilization needs land.

Territory is represented by **Solum**, organised into progressively larger structures and administered by Colonists.

Without territory, there can be no settlement.

→ Learn more: **Territory**

---

### 👥 Colonists

Territory only becomes meaningful when someone administers it.

Colonists acquire, exchange and organise territory, becoming active participants in the history of the civilization.

Without Colonists, the world remains only potential.

→ Learn more: **Colonists**

---

### ⏳ Time

Nothing in Zipvilization becomes mature instantly.

Blocks become time.

Time allows territory to mature.

Mature territory produces population.

History begins to exist.

→ Learn more: **Population & Time**

---

### 📜 Public Rules

Every participant interacts under the same transparent rules.

The smart contract defines the conditions.

The blockchain preserves the history.

The Repository documents the canonical implementation.

→ Learn more: **The Contract**

---

## 🔄 From Rules to Civilization

The contract defines the rules.

The market distributes territory.

Colonists administer that territory.

Time allows it to mature.

Population emerges.

History accumulates.

The civilization becomes observable.

Every layer depends on the previous one.

Nothing is simulated.

Everything follows the same public rules.
