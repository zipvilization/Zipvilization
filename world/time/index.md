# Time

> **On Solum, time is not measured by clocks.**
>
> **It is measured by blocks.**

Zipvilization does not use human time as its primary internal clock.

It uses the blockchain itself.

Every block becomes part of the history of the world.

And groups of blocks form the biological cycles through which Zips can emerge.

This creates a direct relationship between:

    BLOCKCHAIN TIME
    ↓
    BIOLOGICAL TIME
    ↓
    BLOCH
    ↓
    ZIP GENERATION
    ↓
    TERRITORIAL MATURITY
    ↓
    HISTORY

Time is therefore not simply a background variable.

It is one of the structural dimensions of Zipvilization.

---

# Blockchain Time

The blockchain produces blocks continuously.

Those blocks form the fundamental temporal reference of the system.

Zipvilization therefore does not depend on conventional timestamps to define biological development.

Instead:

    BLOCKS
    ↓
    BIOLOGICAL CYCLES

The blockchain itself becomes the clock.

This means that the development of the world is linked directly to the progression of the chain.

---

# The Biological Cycle

The canonical biological cycle of Zipvilization is:

    65,536 BLOCKS

This value defines the fundamental biological unit of time.

A biological cycle is the period required for one valid Bloch process to generate, configure and compress the digital genetic information of one new Zip.

Conceptually:

    65,536 BLOCKS
    =
    1 BIOLOGICAL CYCLE

And under valid conditions:

    1 BIOLOGICAL CYCLE
    ↓
    1 BLOCH PROCESS
    ↓
    1 ZIP

The cycle does not guarantee that a Zip will appear.

It defines the amount of blockchain time required for one valid generation process.

The other canonical conditions must also exist.

---

# Time Alone Does Not Create Zips

This distinction is essential.

The passage of blocks alone does not automatically generate population.

Zip generation requires the simultaneous existence of several conditions.

Conceptually:

    VALID FARM
    +
    ACTIVE TERRITORY
    +
    AVAILABLE CAPACITY
    +
    BIOLOGICAL TIME
    =
    VALID ZIP GENERATION

Therefore:

> **Elapsed blocks alone do not create Zips.**

Time enables the biological process.

It does not replace the territorial conditions required for that process to occur.

---

# Farm-Based Generation

The Farm is the primary population-generating territorial unit.

One valid active Farm can generate:

    1 ZIP
    PER
    BIOLOGICAL CYCLE

while valid unoccupied territorial capacity exists.

Therefore:

    1 FARM
    =
    1 ZIP / CYCLE

This generation rate is fundamental.

Higher territorial levels do not create independent population-generation mechanisms.

Instead, they provide additional capacity that can be populated by the Farms contained within them.

---

# A Farm Begins Empty

A Farm does not need to begin with eight Zips.

Its population can develop from zero.

A newly active Farm may therefore progress like this:

    CYCLE 1
    ↓
    1 ZIP

    CYCLE 2
    ↓
    2 ZIPS

    CYCLE 3
    ↓
    3 ZIPS

    ...

    CYCLE 8
    ↓
    8 ZIPS

At that point the Farm has reached its maximum internal biological capacity.

Therefore:

    FARM CAPACITY
    =
    8 ZIPS

and:

    FARM MATURITY
    =
    8 BIOLOGICAL CYCLES

Since:

    1 CYCLE
    =
    65,536 BLOCKS

then:

    8 × 65,536
    =
    524,288 BLOCKS

So:

> **A Farm reaches biological maturity after 524,288 valid blocks of biological development.**

---

# Farm Maturity Does Not End Generation

A mature Farm has filled its own internal capacity.

But that does not necessarily mean that the Farm stops functioning as a population generator.

If additional valid territorial capacity exists above it, the Farm may continue generating Zips.

For example:

    MATURE FARM
    +
    VALID CITY CAPACITY
    ↓
    CONTINUED GENERATION

The Farm remains the generator.

The City provides additional capacity.

This distinction is fundamental.

---

# Higher Territories Do Not Generate Independently

Cities, States and Kingdoms do not introduce new independent biological generators.

The population-generation mechanism always remains anchored to Farms.

Therefore:

    FARM
    =
    GENERATOR

while:

    CITY
    STATE
    KINGDOM
    =
    ADDITIONAL CAPACITY

This creates an important separation between:

    GENERATION RATE

and:

    TERRITORIAL CAPACITY

The number of Farms determines how quickly population can grow.

The territorial structure determines how much population can exist.

---

# City Development

A City contains:

    256 TILES

Its structural composition is:

    16 COMPLETE FARMS
    +
    128 CITY-LEVEL TILES

The sixteen Farms provide the population-generation mechanism.

When those Farms are mature they contain:

    16 × 8
    =
    128 ZIPS

The City then provides:

    128 ADDITIONAL TILES

which means:

    128 ADDITIONAL ZIP CAPACITY

The sixteen Farms continue generating population.

Their combined generation rate is:

    16 FARMS
    =
    16 ZIPS / CYCLE

Therefore:

    128 ADDITIONAL ZIPS
    ÷
    16 ZIPS / CYCLE
    =
    8 ADDITIONAL CYCLES

So the City requires:

    8 FARM CYCLES
    +
    8 CITY-LEVEL CYCLES
    =
    16 CUMULATIVE CYCLES

In blocks:

    16 × 65,536
    =
    1,048,576 BLOCKS

Therefore:

> **A fully populated City represents 16 cumulative biological cycles.**

---

# State Development

A State contains:

    8,192 TILES

Its structural composition is:

    16 COMPLETE CITIES
    +
    4,096 STATE-LEVEL TILES

Those sixteen Cities contain:

    256 FARMS

The population-generation rate is therefore:

    256 ZIPS / CYCLE

The State introduces:

    4,096 ADDITIONAL TILES

which means:

    4,096 ADDITIONAL ZIP CAPACITY

Therefore:

    4,096
    ÷
    256
    =
    16 ADDITIONAL CYCLES

The cumulative biological development becomes:

    16 PREVIOUS CYCLES
    +
    16 STATE-LEVEL CYCLES
    =
    32 CUMULATIVE CYCLES

In blocks:

    32 × 65,536
    =
    2,097,152 BLOCKS

Therefore:

> **A fully populated State represents 32 cumulative biological cycles.**

---

# Kingdom Development

A Kingdom contains:

    262,144 TILES

Its structural composition is:

    16 COMPLETE STATES
    +
    131,072 KINGDOM-LEVEL TILES

Those States contain:

    4,096 FARMS

The combined generation rate is therefore:

    4,096 ZIPS / CYCLE

The Kingdom introduces:

    131,072 ADDITIONAL TILES

which means:

    131,072 ADDITIONAL ZIP CAPACITY

Therefore:

    131,072
    ÷
    4,096
    =
    32 ADDITIONAL CYCLES

The cumulative biological development becomes:

    32 PREVIOUS CYCLES
    +
    32 KINGDOM-LEVEL CYCLES
    =
    64 CUMULATIVE CYCLES

In blocks:

    64 × 65,536
    =
    4,194,304 BLOCKS

Therefore:

> **A fully populated Kingdom represents 64 cumulative biological cycles.**

# Generation Rate and Capacity

Population development depends on two different variables.

## Generation Rate

Determined by the number of valid generating Farms.

Example:

    1 FARM
    =
    1 ZIP / CYCLE

    16 FARMS
    =
    16 ZIPS / CYCLE

    256 FARMS
    =
    256 ZIPS / CYCLE

    4,096 FARMS
    =
    4,096 ZIPS / CYCLE

## Capacity

Determined by the total valid Territory.

Example:

    FARM
    =
    8 ZIP CAPACITY

    CITY
    =
    256 ZIP CAPACITY

    STATE
    =
    8,192 ZIP CAPACITY

    KINGDOM
    =
    262,144 ZIP CAPACITY

Therefore:

> **Farms determine how fast population can grow. Territory determines how far population can grow.**

Bloch and Time are closely connected but they are not the same concept.

Bloch defines the digital-genetic process.

Time defines the duration of that process.

Therefore:

    BLOCH
    =
    MECHANISM

while:

    BIOLOGICAL CYCLE
    =
    TIME

Conceptually:

    BLOCH
    +
    65,536 BLOCKS
    +
    VALID TERRITORIAL CONDITIONS
    ↓
    UNIQUE ZIP

The blockchain provides the temporal substrate.

Bloch provides the biological mechanism.

Territory provides the capacity.

The Farm provides the generation rate.

# Time and Territory

Territory determines biological capacity.

Time determines how that capacity may become populated.

Therefore:

    TERRITORY
    =
    SPACE

    TIME
    =
    DEVELOPMENT

Neither is sufficient alone.

A large Territory without enough elapsed biological Time may remain sparsely populated.

A long period of elapsed Time without valid territorial capacity cannot generate population beyond that capacity.

This creates a direct relationship between:

    SPACE
    ×
    TIME
    =
    DEVELOPMENT POSSIBILITY

But the actual result depends on valid History.

# Time and History

Time becomes meaningful through History.

The blockchain does not merely tell Zipvilization what exists now.

It also records how the current state was reached.

This distinction is essential because biological maturity cannot be determined from current balances alone.

For example:

A Colonist may currently hold enough SOLUM for a City.

But that does not mean the City has existed long enough to become biologically mature.

Similarly:

A Colonist may once have held a mature City and later reduce the Territory.

The historical biological development that already occurred does not disappear simply because the current balance changed.

Therefore:

> **Current Territory does not equal historical Territory.**

And:

> **Current capacity does not equal historical maturity.**

---

# Historical Reconstruction

To determine the biological state of Zipvilization correctly, the system must reconstruct territorial history.

Conceptually:

    BLOCKCHAIN HISTORY
    ↓
    SOLUM THROUGH TIME
    ↓
    TILES THROUGH TIME
    ↓
    FARMS THROUGH TIME
    ↓
    VALID ACTIVE / GENERATING FARMS
    ↓
    AVAILABLE HIGHER TERRITORIAL CAPACITY
    ↓
    VALID BIOLOGICAL CYCLES
    ↓
    ZIP GENERATION
    ↓
    TERRITORIAL MATURITY

This means that maturity is not simply a calculation performed on the present.

It is a reconstruction of what actually happened.

---

# The Farm as Historical Reference

The Farm is the primary historical reference for biological development.

Why?

Because the Farm is the population generator.

Higher territories provide additional capacity, but their biological development depends on the Farms contained within them.

Therefore:

    FARM HISTORY
    ↓
    POPULATION HISTORY
    ↓
    HIGHER TERRITORIAL MATURITY

This allows the system to reconstruct population growth deterministically.

---

# Later Territory Does Not Rewrite Earlier Time

Suppose a Colonist begins with one Farm.

The Farm develops for several biological cycles.

Later, the Colonist acquires enough SOLUM to form a City.

The new City capacity becomes available from that point forward.

It does not retroactively exist during the earlier Farm period.

Therefore:

    LATER TERRITORY
    ≠
    RETROACTIVE CAPACITY

Similarly:

    LATER PURCHASE
    ≠
    RETROACTIVE POPULATION

The blockchain history determines when capacity actually became available.

---

# Selling Territory Does Not Erase History

The opposite is also true.

Suppose a Colonist develops a Territory over a long period and later sells part of the SOLUM.

The current territorial structure may change.

But the historical development that occurred while the Territory existed remains part of Zipvilization.

Therefore:

    CURRENT STATE
    MAY CHANGE

while:

    VALID PAST EVENTS
    REMAIN HISTORY

This principle is fundamental.

> **A transfer changes future state. It does not rewrite history.**

---

# Time Creates Difference

Two Territories with identical current size may have completely different histories.

For example:

    TERRITORY A
    =
    1 CITY
    =
    256 TILES

    TERRITORY B
    =
    1 CITY
    =
    256 TILES

Their current capacity may be identical.

But one may have existed for many biological cycles while the other may have formed recently.

Therefore:

    SAME CAPACITY
    ≠
    SAME MATURITY

and:

    SAME TERRITORY
    ≠
    SAME HISTORY

This is one of the mechanisms through which Territories may become differentiated over time.

---

# Time Creates Irreversibility

Because valid historical events remain part of History, time introduces irreversibility into Zipvilization.

Once a valid event has occurred:

    IT HAPPENED

Future changes may alter the state of the world.

They do not erase the fact that the event occurred.

This means that Zipvilization develops a persistent historical structure.

The world does not simply recompute itself from the present.

It remembers.

But population development still depends on:

- valid generating Farms,
- available capacity,
- biological cycles,
- and historical progression.

Therefore:

> **Territorial scale can change quickly. Biological maturity cannot be retroactively created.**
