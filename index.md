## Territory is capacity, not population

The territorial system begins with a simple relationship:

> **1 SOLUM = 1 m² of Solum**

From there, Territory can form progressively larger structures:

**Tile → Farm → City → State → Kingdom**

But Territory does not automatically create population.

> **capacity ≠ population**

Population requires valid territorial state, generation conditions and Time.

A Holder is also not automatically a Colonist.

> **Holder ≠ Colonist**

And current state cannot reconstruct the entire past by itself.

> **Current state ≠ complete History**

## A world with state
