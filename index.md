# Can a digital civilization emerge?

Welcome to **Zipvilization**.

Zipvilization is an open experiment.

Its purpose is not to build another blockchain project, another token or another investment platform.

Its purpose is to explore whether a civilization can emerge from **public rules, shared territory, time and participation**.

This Atlas has been created for one reason.

**To help Humans and Artificial Intelligence understand that experiment.**

Not to convince.

Not to sell.

To understand.
