# 1. DOCUMENT MODEL

The repository uses five primary semantic layers.

    HISTORY
    question: What happened?
    function: preserve events, sequence, decisions, anomalies, development
    authority_domain: historical record

    EPISTEMIC_STATUS
    question: What kind of knowledge is this?
    function: classify certainty, evidence, interpretation, and openness
    authority_domain: knowledge status

    RELATIONSHIPS
    question: What must remain connected?
    function: preserve structural connections
    authority_domain: relational structure

    CANON
    question: What must remain true?
    function: preserve identity continuity and established invariants
    authority_domain: active invariants

    HORIZONTE
    question: Where should development remain oriented?
    function: preserve direction without defining destination
    authority_domain: future orientation

Sources:

    HISTORY          -> /history/
    EPISTEMIC_STATUS -> /epistemic-status/
    RELATIONSHIPS    -> /relationships/
    CANON            -> /canon/
    HORIZONTE        -> /horizonte/

These layers are complementary.

They are not interchangeable.

# 2. AUTHORITY IS DOMAIN-SPECIFIC

There is no universal rule:

    DOCUMENT_A > DOCUMENT_B

Use:

    historical event          -> HISTORY

    structural connection     -> RELATIONSHIPS

    future orientation        -> HORIZONTE

    Did event X happen?

    Resolve primarily through:
    ACTIVE CANON

    HORIZONTE
