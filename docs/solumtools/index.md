# 🛠️ SolumTools  
**On-chain Signals → Readable Reality**

SolumTools is the **interpretation layer** of Solum.

It does not change the blockchain.  
It does not control behavior.  
It does not hide information.

SolumTools exists to **observe**, **extract**, and **translate** on-chain activity into a form that can later be understood as territory, participation, and history.

---

## 🌱 What SolumTools Is

SolumTools is a set of tools and definitions that:

- Read **public on-chain data** from the [Solum](../solum/) contract and its market interface.
- Extract **structural signals** (supply, movement, limits, fees, treasury flows).
- Normalize those signals into **coherent, deterministic states**.
- Prepare the data for their interpretative mirrors in [SolumWorld](../solumworld/) and [SolumView](../solumview/).

SolumTools is not narrative.

It is **pre-narrative**.

---

## 🔍 What SolumTools Does

SolumTools focuses on **visibility and structure**, not judgment.

It enables:

- 📊 Observation of **any wallet / territory**
- 🔎 Search and inspection of **any colonist**
- 📐 Measurement of territory size, age, and activity
- ⏳ Tracking of historical evolution over time
- 🧱 Classification of roles based on **verifiable behavioral signals**

Everything SolumTools reads is already public on-chain.

SolumTools only makes it **legible**.

---

## 🧭 Roles Without Judgment

SolumTools does not define *good* or *bad* actors.

Every on-chain action has a **function** within the system.

Examples of observable roles:

- Veteran colonists (early or persistent holders)
- Virgin territories (untouched land)
- Highly active territories
- Major landholders
- Contributors to burn, liquidity, or reflection dynamics

All roles coexist.

All contribute to the system's evolution.

---

## 🌍 Open World by Design

SolumTools is **open by default**:

- Any wallet can be observed
- Any territory can be inspected
- No private views
- No hidden dashboards

Transparency is not a feature.

It is the foundation.

---

## 🚫 What SolumTools Is NOT

SolumTools is **not**:

- A game
- A strategy interface
- A financial trading dashboard
- A governance system
- A simulation of Zipvilization itself

It does not render the world.

It does not assign meaning.

That happens **one layer above**, in [SolumWorld](../solumworld/) and its visual expression through [SolumView](../solumview/).

---

## 🔗 Relationship to Zipvilization

SolumTools has two canonical mirrors:

- **[SolumWorld](../solumworld/)** — where signals become a coherent, rule-bound world state.
- **[SolumView](../solumview/)** — where that world is expressed visually and temporally.

In this process:

- Wallets become **territories**
- Holders become **colonists**
- On-chain mechanics acquire **physical meaning**

The interpretation itself is defined by Zipvilization, while the underlying signals always originate from [Solum](../solum/).

---

## 📐 Technical Grounding

SolumTools is grounded entirely in **verifiable on-chain data**.

All extracted signals derive from:

- Fixed supply and real burn mechanics
- Liquidity pool state
- Treasury inflows
- Reflection dynamics
- Transaction and wallet constraints
- Historical transaction logs

The underlying mechanics are defined by the [Solum Contract Specification](../solum/CONTRACT_SPEC.md).

If a signal cannot be derived on-chain, it does not exist in SolumTools.

---

## 🧱 Position in the System

SolumTools sits **between**:

- 🔒 **[Solum](../solum/)** — immutable on-chain substrate
- 🌍 **[SolumWorld](../solumworld/)** — canonical world/state layer
- 🎨 **[SolumView](../solumview/)** — visual and temporal expression

It does not decide meaning.

It makes meaning **possible**.

---

## 🧠 Core Principle

SolumTools assumes one thing:

> If a civilization is real, it must be observable.

No filters.  
No shortcuts.  
No moral layers.

Only signals, structure, and time.
