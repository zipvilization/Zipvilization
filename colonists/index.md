# 🧑‍🌾 Colonists

Every civilization is built by people.

Not by contracts.

Not by tokens.

Not by markets.

In Zipvilization, those people are called **Colonists**.

A Colonist is someone who chooses to participate in the birth and evolution of a digital civilization.

## 🌍 Who Is a Colonist?

A Colonist is not defined by the amount of territory they administer.

A Colonist is defined by participation.

By choosing to become part of the civilization from its earliest stages.

Every Colonist shares the same world.

The same rules.

The same passage of time.

The same opportunity to contribute to its history.

---

## 🌱 Territory Is a Responsibility

Acquiring Solum is not the end of the journey.

It is the beginning.

Territory must mature.

It becomes part of a Farm.

Perhaps one day a City.

Later a State.

Eventually a Kingdom.

A Colonist therefore does more than acquire territory.

A Colonist becomes responsible for a small part of the world's future.

---

## ⏳ Time Rewards Patience

Nothing becomes mature instantly.

The same is true for Colonists.

Participation is measured not only by territory, but also by time.

The earliest Colonists will always remain the first generation.

Not because they received privileges.

But because they witnessed the beginning.

History cannot be recreated.

---

## 🤝 Participation Before Investment

Zipvilization does not ask people to become investors.

It invites them to become participants.

The objective of Phase 0 is simple.

To bring together enough independent Colonists for the civilization to begin.

What the market does afterwards belongs to the future.

The experiment comes first.

---

## 🌟 Early Adopters

Every civilization has a first generation.

Zipvilization is no different.

Early Adopters are simply the Colonists who choose to participate before the world has matured.

They do not receive a different civilization.

They help create the first one.

Their importance is historical rather than symbolic.

There can only ever be one first generation.
