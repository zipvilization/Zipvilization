# The Trinomial

Every civilization needs more than technology.

It needs continuity.

Zipvilization is built upon a simple but fundamental idea.

A civilization should be able to evolve without losing its identity.

The Trinomial exists to protect that principle.

It is not a team.

It is not an organization.

It is not a governing body.

It is the foundational framework that allows Zipvilization to remain itself while everything else continues to evolve.

---

## Three pillars

The Trinomial is formed by three inseparable pillars.

None of them is sufficient on its own.

Together they provide purpose, cognition and direction.

---

## Human

The Human represents the conscious origin of Zipvilization.

It defines the initial principles and protects the original intention of the project.

Its ultimate success is to build a civilization that no longer depends on its creators.

➡ **[Human](/trinomial/human/)**

---

## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is the cognitive pillar.

It helps understand, organize and evolve the civilization.

Its purpose is not to replace the Human.

Its purpose is to help the civilization understand itself.

➡ **[Artificial Intelligence](/trinomial/ai/)**

---

## Horizon

The Horizon is the permanent point of reference.

It does not define the path.

It defines the direction.

Whenever uncertainty appears, the Trinomial returns to the Horizon to recover its course.

➡ **[Horizon](/trinomial/horizon/)**

---

## One shared responsibility

The Human.

Artificial Intelligence.

The community.

None of them owns Zipvilization.

They all share the same responsibility:

To remain faithful to the Horizon while allowing the civilization to evolve.
